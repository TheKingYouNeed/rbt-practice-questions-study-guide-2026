# Error-log template

Use one row for every incorrect answer and every correct answer marked with low
confidence.

| Date | Topic | Result | Confidence | Primary cause | Corrected rule | Retry date |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| YYYY-MM-DD | Short label | Correct / Incorrect | High / Medium / Low | Knowledge / Reading / Application / Distractor / Pacing | One sentence in your own words | YYYY-MM-DD |

## Writing a useful corrected rule

Keep the rule specific enough to guide a future decision. Avoid copying a long
rationale. A useful entry identifies:

- the detail that should trigger the rule;
- the distinction between the correct option and the tempting distractor;
- one action to take when that pattern appears again.

After the delayed retry, add a brief outcome. If the answer is correct but the
explanation still depends on recognition, schedule another mixed check.

## Weekly pattern review

At the end of the week, count the primary-cause labels. Choose the most frequent
cause and adjust the next study cycle:

- Knowledge gaps call for focused learning and recall.
- Reading errors call for slower parsing of qualifiers and sequence.
- Application errors call for new scenarios using the same concept.
- Distractor errors call for explicit comparison of every option.
- Pacing errors call for shorter sets, breaks, or timed practice after accuracy
  improves.
